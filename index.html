About Us

At Salgaonkar Arts, we carry forward a proud family tradition of idol-making, blending artistry with devotion. Each piece is lovingly sculpted and painted by hand, capturing divine beauty in every detail.

From traditional Ganesh and Krishna idols to Naag Panchami snake idols and custom thermocol makars, our pieces are designed to bring grace to your celebration while caring for nature.

Eco-friendly materials • Handcrafted with love • Bulk & custom orders welcome
